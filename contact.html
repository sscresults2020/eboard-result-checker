Before you write to us

We cannot look up, change or explain an individual result. We are not an education board and we have no access to anyone's result records, marks or certificates. If your result looks wrong, or a subject is missing, contact your own institution or your education board directly, they are the only ones who can act on it. Please do not send us your roll number, registration number or any personal documents.
