GUIDEHow SSC GPA Is Calculated in Bangladesh

The SSC GPA runs on a five point scale, from 1.00 up to a maximum of 5.00. Every subject earns a grade point based on the marks scored, and your GPA is the average of those points across your main subjects, with one important twist for the optional 4th subject.

The grade point scale

MarksLetter gradeGrade point
80 to 100A+5.00
70 to 79A4.00
60 to 69A minus3.50
50 to 59B3.00
40 to 49C2.00
33 to 39D1.00
0 to 32F0.00

The 4th subject rule

This is the part most students want to be sure about. Your 4th subject is an optional or additional subject. It does not count as a full main subject in the average. Instead, whatever grade point you earn in it above 2.00 (grade C) is added to your total as a bonus. So if you score A+ (5.00) in your 4th subject, then 5.00 minus 2.00, which is 3.00 bonus points, are added to the sum of your main subject points before dividing.

In short, the formula is: add up the grade points of your main subjects, add the bonus from the 4th subject, then divide by the number of main subjects. The result is capped at 5.00, so a very strong 4th subject cannot push you past the maximum.

When the result is a fail

One rule overrides everything else. If you score an F in any main subject, the overall result is a fail regardless of how high your other grades are. The 4th subject is the exception; a failing grade there does not fail you overall, it simply adds no bonus. This calculator follows the same logic, so an F in a main subject will show a Fail status.

A note on accuracy: this tool gives your expected GPA based on the grades you enter. Your official result always comes from the education board. Once results are published on 10 August 2026, check the real figure on your board result page or an official portal.
