About Us

An independent information portal for SSC, Dakhil and Vocational results in Bangladesh.

Last updated · 25 July 2026

Exam Results BD is an independent Bangladeshi website that helps students, parents and teachers follow the SSC Result 2026 — when it will be published, how to check it, and what to do after the result is out.

What this website does

Every year, close to two million students sit for the SSC, Dakhil and SSC Vocational examinations under eleven education boards. On result day, the same questions are asked by everyone at once: what time will the result come out, how do I read my marksheet, what is the SMS format for my board, and what happens if I want my script rechecked.

We put the answers to those questions in one place, written in plain English, and keep them updated as the boards release new information. Specifically, we cover:

  • The SSC Result 2026 published date and time, updated whenever the Ministry of Education or the boards announce a change.
  • Step-by-step instructions for checking your result online with your roll and registration number.
  • The SMS method and the three-letter code for each education board.
  • How to view and save the full marksheet with subject-wise grades and GPA.
  • The re-scrutiny (board challenge) process, its deadline and its fees.
  • Board-wise information for Dhaka, Rajshahi, Chattogram, Cumilla, Jashore, Sylhet, Barishal, Dinajpur, Mymensingh, Madrasah and Technical.

We are not an education board

Important. Exam Results BD is a private, independent website. We are not the Ministry of Education, we are not any education board, and we are not connected to the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee. We do not issue, hold, change or certify anyone's result. Your official result comes from the education board alone.

The official result portals are educationboardresults.gov.bd and eboardresults.com. If anything on this website ever differs from what the board publishes, the board's record is the correct one and ours is not.

How we work

Where our information comes from

We follow announcements from the Ministry of Education and the education boards, notices published on official board websites, and reporting from established Bangladeshi news outlets. When a date or a figure is not yet confirmed, we say so on the page rather than presenting a guess as fact.

Keeping pages current

Exam news changes quickly, and a result date can move more than once. We update the affected pages when it does, and the announcement bar at the top of the site carries the latest status so you can see at a glance whether the result has been published.

Corrections

If you find something on this site that is out of date or wrong, please tell us. We would rather fix it quickly than leave a student acting on bad information. Write to us at contact@examresultsbd.com and mention the page and what needs correcting.

How the site is funded

This website is free to use. Running costs are covered by advertising displayed on the pages, including advertising served by third-party networks. Advertising never decides what we publish, and no advertiser is given influence over our content. You can read more in our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

A note to students

Whatever your result is, it is one exam. A GPA opens some doors and closes others, but it does not decide the rest of your life. If your result is not what you hoped for, look at the re-scrutiny option, talk to your teachers and family, and take the next step calmly.
