SECTION 01Sylhet Education Board: An Overview

The Sylhet Education Board, established in 1999, is the youngest of the general boards and serves the greater Sylhet region in the north-east, covering the four districts of Sylhet, Moulvibazar, Habiganj and Sunamganj. Though it is the smallest general board by candidate numbers, it serves a distinct region with strong ties to the Bangladeshi diaspora and a growing education sector.

Sylhet city anchors the region academically, with respected institutions and a strong network of colleges. For a Sylhet Board student, the SSC result is the first step toward college admission within the division, and a strong GPA keeps options open both locally and for competitive colleges in Dhaka.

The result was first set for 20 July 2026, then postponed; the boards have confirmed publication on 10 August 2026, released at 2:00 PM with every board. A short delay does not change your marks, so rely only on official Sylhet Board notices rather than unofficial dates. We update this page the moment a firm date is confirmed.

SECTION 03How to Check SSC Result 2026 Sylhet Board Online

The online method shows the full marksheet with a grade in every subject. Keep your admit card (roll) and registration card ready.

  1. Open an official portal. Use eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or sylhetboard.gov.bd.
  2. Select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Sylhet as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet straight away.

If the site is slow, stay calm. Heavy traffic at 2:00 PM slows the servers for everyone. Refresh after a moment, or use SMS below, which does not depend on the website.

SECTION 04Check Sylhet Board Result 2026 by SMS

SMS works on any handset with no internet. Send this to 16222:

SSC SYL <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your roll number. For roll 123456 you would send SSC SYL 123456 2026 to 16222 and receive your GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Prepare it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts.

SECTION 05Sylhet Board SSC Result 2025: The Numbers

Last year is your clearest guide. In 2025, Sylhet recorded a pass rate of 68.57 percent. About 1,02,219 students appeared, 70,091 passed, and 3,614 earned GPA 5. Unusually, the split was very close: 1,791 male and 1,823 female GPA 5 holders, and the male pass rate (68.62 percent) edged the female rate (68.54 percent).

SECTION 07Sylhet Board Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

For the subject-wise marksheet with number and grade, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page then lists each subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your overall GPA. For complete instructions, see our dedicated guide on getting the Sylhet Board result with marksheet.

Beyond the headline GPA, the marksheet is worth reading in full. It shows the grade you earned in every subject, which tells you where you were strong and where a subject dragged the average down. That detail matters for the group and college you target next, and it is the same sheet colleges look at during admission, so take a moment to understand every line rather than glancing only at the final number.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

The number everyone looks at first is the GPA, scored from 1.00 to 5.00, where the top grade of GPA 5.00 is what students call A+. Each subject carries a letter grade and a matching grade point, and your GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted specially: points above a grade C in the 4th subject are added to your total, which is how many students reach a full 5.00. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, meaning grade point 1.00, in every compulsory and elective subject. One F pulls that subject into fail, so read every row, not just the final figure.

SECTION 08If Your Sylhet Result Looks Wrong

Should a subject look lower than you expected, you may apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. For the full process, fees and dates, read our guide on the SSC board challenge and re-scrutiny. It re-checks the marking mechanics, whether every answer was marked and the marks added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only for subjects you genuinely doubt, since most applications do not change a grade.

Common mistakes to avoid on result day

A few simple errors trip up Sylhet Board students every year, and all of them are easy to sidestep. First, entering the wrong registration number: the roll and registration are different numbers, and the full marksheet will not open without the correct registration, so check your registration card before you start. Second, trusting unofficial mirror sites that promise a faster result; they may show wrong data or misuse the roll and registration you type in, so stay on the official portals or a trusted checker. Third, giving up when the site stalls at 2:00 PM; that is congestion, not a missing result, and the SMS route to 16222 sidesteps it entirely. Finally, forgetting to save the marksheet once it loads; take a screenshot and use the print option immediately, because you will need it for college admission and the servers may slow again later in the day.

SECTION 09After the Result: College Admission

For most Sylhet Board students, the result quickly becomes a question of which college next? Class XI admission runs through a central online system that opens soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your group and subject grades. Build a shortlist in three tiers, a few ambitious names such as the leading colleges of Sylhet city, several realistic choices, and a couple of safe options, and be ready to apply the moment the circular appears.
