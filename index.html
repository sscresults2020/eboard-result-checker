The Secondary School Certificate, or SSC, is one of the two great public examinations of Bangladeshi school life, taken at the end of Class 10 under the country's education boards. For most students it is the first exam that feels genuinely national in scale — the same papers, the same days, the same nervous wait, shared with nearly two million others. This year, a total of 18,57,344 candidates registered across the 11 boards, split almost evenly between 9,30,305 male and 9,27,039 female students, sitting from 30,666 institutions at 3,885 centres nationwide. Of those, roughly 14.2 lakh sat under the general boards, about 3 lakh under the Madrasah Board for Dakhil, and the remainder under the Technical Board. Whichever group you belong to, this guide covers your result.

01SSC Result 2026 Date and Time

Here is the short version, because it is the one thing everyone opens this page for. The SSC and equivalent examinations of 2026 began on 21 April 2026, and the written papers under the nine general boards finished on 20 May 2026. The results were originally announced for 20 July 2026. That date has now been postponed, and the boards expect to publish in the last week of July 2026, with most reporting pointing to on or shortly after 28–29 July.

On a normal result day the process follows a set order. The Education Minister and the board chairmen hand a summary of the results to the head of government, a short press briefing shares the national pass rate and GPA-5 figures, and then — at 2:00 PM — the results go live for everyone at once. All 11 education boards publish simultaneously, and the SMS service switches on at the same minute. In 2025 the handover ceremony was skipped and boards released figures independently, so the ceremony itself can change year to year; the 2:00 PM online release is the part that stays consistent.

02Why the SSC Result 2026 Was Delayed

The original 20 July date came from the Education Minister at a press conference on 8 June 2026. So why the change? According to the President of the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee, the boards simply could not finish evaluating and compiling results in time, citing recent disruption among the reasons. Their message was straightforward: the preparation would be completed within about a week, after which the ministry would set the new date.

“We were instructed to prepare the results by July 20, but could not complete the work for several reasons. The final decision on the publication date rests with the ministry.” — Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee, reported July 2026

For students, a short delay changes nothing about the outcome — your marks are your marks. What it does mean is a few more days of waiting, and a good reason to be careful about which “date” you trust. Every year, a wave of pages and social posts announce a confident result date that turns out to be a guess. Rely on official board notices and established news outlets, and ignore screenshots that circulate without a source.

03How to Check SSC Result 2026 Online

Checking online is the most popular method because it shows your full marksheet with subject-wise grades, not just a pass/fail line. You will need two things from your exam documents: your 6-digit roll number (on your admit card) and your 10-digit registration number (on your registration card). Keep both beside you before you start.

  1. Open an official portal Go to eboardresults.com or educationboardresults.gov.bd. The checker at the top of this page also opens the official portal for you.
  2. Select the examination Choose SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent (or SSC Vocational if that is your exam).
  3. Select the year Choose 2026.
  4. Select your board Pick the board printed on your admit card — Dhaka, Rajshahi, Chattogram, and so on.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number Type them carefully. The roll alone shows the basic result; both together unlock the full marksheet.
  6. Solve the security check Answer the simple sum or captcha shown — this stops automated abuse of the server.
  7. Press “Get Result” Your result appears on screen. Take a screenshot or use the print option to save a copy.

Server busy on result day? This is completely normal. Nearly two million students refresh the same site within minutes of 2:00 PM, and the official servers slow down under that load. Do not panic and do not enter your details on random “mirror” sites. Wait a few minutes, refresh with Ctrl + R, or use the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website at all.

04How to Check SSC Result 2026 by SMS

The SMS method is the quiet hero of result day. It needs no internet, works on the simplest phone, and keeps working when the website is crawling. It costs a small amount per message (standard operator charge), and it delivers your GPA and pass status straight to your inbox.

Open your phone's message app and send a message in this format to 16222:

SSC  <BOARD>  <ROLL>  2026

The <BOARD> part is the first three letters of your board's English name. For example, a Dhaka Board student with roll 123456 would type SSC DHA 123456 2026 and send it to 16222. Within a short while, a return SMS arrives with the result. For Dakhil (madrasah) students, replace SSC with DAKHIL; for SSC Vocational, use SSC TEC as shown in the table below.

06SSC Result 2026 with Marksheet (Subject-Wise Number)

Most students want more than a GPA — they want the full marksheet, showing the grade in every subject. This is where the online method wins, because SMS only returns your GPA and pass status.

To see your SSC result 2026 with marksheet, use the online steps in section 3 and make sure you enter both your roll and registration number. The individual result page then displays each subject with its letter grade and grade point, along with your overall GPA. From there you can screenshot it or use your browser's print option to save a PDF copy for college admission.

Grade sheet vs. certificate. The marksheet you see online right after results is the provisional grade sheet. The original printed grade sheet and certificate are distributed later through your school or college. For class XI admission, the online grade sheet is accepted — you do not need to wait for the printed copy to start applying.

What your marksheet actually shows

When the full result loads, you are looking at more than a single number. A typical SSC marksheet lists your name, roll and registration, your board and institution, your group (science, humanities or business studies), and then the heart of it: a row for every subject with its letter grade and grade point. Compulsory subjects like Bangla and English appear alongside your group subjects, and your optional 4th subject is listed too. At the bottom sits your overall GPA and your result status.

It is worth checking each row carefully rather than jumping straight to the GPA. Occasionally a student spots a subject that looks lower than expected, and that single row is exactly the kind of thing the re-scrutiny process (section 9) exists to re-check. Read the whole sheet before you decide whether you are happy with it.

Saving and printing your marksheet

Once your marksheet is on screen, save it immediately — result-day servers are unpredictable and you do not want to be hunting for it again an hour later. On a phone, a screenshot is fastest; on a computer, use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF. Keep a copy in your email or cloud storage as well, because you will be uploading or showing this grade sheet repeatedly during college admission.

07Understanding Your GPA and Grades

Bangladesh uses a Grade Point Average (GPA) system on a scale from 1.00 to 5.00. The top grade, GPA 5.00, is what everyone calls A+ ("A plus"). Here is how the letter grades, marks and grade points line up:

Marks (%)Letter GradeGrade PointMeaning
80–100A+5.00Outstanding
70–79A4.00Excellent
60–69A−3.50Very good
50–59B3.00Good
40–49C2.00Average
33–39D1.00Pass
0–32F0.00Fail

Your final GPA is the average of the grade points across your subjects, with a small twist: the 4th subject (optional subject) is counted in a special way. If you score above grade C in your 4th subject, the points above 2.00 are added to your total — which is how many students push their GPA up to a full 5.00. To pass overall, you must earn at least a grade D (grade point 1.00) in every compulsory and elective subject; an F in even one subject means the overall result is recorded as failed for that subject.

09The 11 Education Boards Explained

Bangladesh does not have one national result — it has eleven boards publishing together on the same day. Nine are general education boards defined by region, and two are specialised: the Madrasah Education Board, which conducts the Dakhil exam (the madrasah equivalent of SSC), and the Bangladesh Technical Education Board, which runs SSC Vocational. Knowing which board you belong to matters, because your board code, your notices and sometimes your admission timing all follow from it.

10SSC Re-scrutiny 2026 (Board Challenge)

If your result in a subject feels wrong — a grade lower than you were sure of — you can apply for re-scrutiny, often called a board challenge. It is important to understand what this actually is: the board does not re-mark your paper from scratch. Instead, it re-checks the mechanics — whether every answer was marked, whether the marks were added correctly, and whether the total was carried over properly. In practice, addition and omission errors are the most common reasons a grade changes.

How to apply

  1. Wait for the application window It opens the day after results and usually stays open for about a week. The exact dates come in the official notice.
  2. Send an SMS from Teletalk Re-scrutiny applications are made by SMS in the format announced by the board (starting with RSC), using a Teletalk SIM.
  3. Pay the fee per subject The fee is typically around 125–150 Taka per subject/paper. Choose only the subjects you genuinely doubt — the cost adds up.
  4. Wait for the review result The re-scrutiny result is usually published about a month after the application deadline, on the same official portals.

Be realistic. Most applications do not change a grade, because the marking itself is not re-done. Apply where you have a real reason — a subject you were confident about that came back surprisingly low — rather than challenging every paper out of hope.

11College (Class XI) Admission After SSC

For most students, the SSC result exists to answer one practical question: which college can I get into? Class XI admission in Bangladesh runs through a central online system, and it opens not long after results are published — which is exactly why a delayed result compresses the gap and makes it important to be ready in advance.

Admission is competitive and largely merit-based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your subject grades within your group. Higher-demand colleges fill their merit lists with the top GPAs first, so your realistic target depends on both your own result and the national picture that year. This is where the pass-rate trend matters: in a low-pass-rate year, a strong GPA stands out more; in a high one, the competition at the top is fierce.

How to prepare before the circular

  • Keep your grade sheet saved as a PDF and know your exact GPA and subject grades.
  • Build a shortlist of colleges in three tiers — a few ambitious, several realistic, and a couple of safe options you would still be happy with.
  • Check each college's group and GPA requirements so you do not waste choices on colleges you cannot qualify for.
  • Have your details and a photo ready for the online application the moment the admission circular is published.

Don't lose the result-day momentum. Every year some students celebrate (or sulk) for a week and then scramble when admission opens. Treat result day and admission as one connected process: the day you have your GPA, start planning your college list.

12What to Do After the SSC Result

The result is not the finish line — it is the starting gun for the next stage. Here is what comes after, whichever way your result goes.

If you passed

Congratulations — now move quickly, because class XI (college) admission opens soon after results through the central online admission system. Your SSC GPA and subject grades decide which colleges you can realistically target. Make a shortlist that mixes ambitious choices with safe ones, keep your grade sheet and a passport photo ready, and watch the official admission circular for dates. A great result means little if you miss the admission window.

If the result was not what you hoped

First, breathe. A single exam does not define your intelligence or your future, and plenty of people who stumbled at SSC went on to do very well. Your practical options include applying for re-scrutiny in subjects you doubt (section 9), and if you did not pass, sitting the next available exam to improve. Talk to your teachers about where things went wrong and how to prepare differently. The result is information, not a verdict.

Whatever the number on the screen, it is one measurement on one day. It opens some doors and closes others — it does not decide the whole building.

13Result-Day Tips (So You Don't Panic)

  • Keep both numbers ready. Roll and registration, written down or photographed, before 2:00 PM.
  • Draft your SMS in advance. Type SSC DHA 123456 2026 (your details) and keep it ready to send at 2:00 PM sharp.
  • Expect the website to be slow. It is not broken — it is busy. Refresh calmly or switch to SMS.
  • Only use official portals. Never enter your details on unknown “fast result” sites that ask for personal information.
  • Save a copy immediately. Screenshot or print your marksheet the moment it loads — you will need it for admission.
  • Don't compare obsessively. Your friend's GPA is not your scoreboard. Focus on your own next step.

14Frequently Asked Questions

When will the SSC Result 2026 be published?

The SSC Result 2026 was originally set for 20 July 2026 but has been postponed. The education boards expect to publish it in the last week of July 2026, around 28–29 July, with the Ministry of Education confirming the final date. Results are traditionally released at 2:00 PM.

How can I check my SSC result with the full marksheet?

Visit an official portal such as eboardresults.com or educationboardresults.gov.bd, select SSC, choose 2026 and your board, then enter both your roll number and registration number. Entering both is what displays the subject-wise marksheet rather than just the basic result.

Can I check my result with only the roll number?

Yes. You can see your basic result and GPA with just the roll number, and by SMS you only need the roll. However, the full marksheet with subject-wise grades requires both the roll number and the registration number.

What is the SMS format for the SSC Result 2026?

Send SSC <BOARD> <ROLL> 2026 to 16222. For example, a Dhaka Board student types SSC DHA 123456 2026. Dakhil students start with DAKHIL and use MAD; vocational students use TEC. See the board-code table above for every board.

How many education boards publish the SSC result?

Eleven boards publish simultaneously: nine general education boards (Dhaka, Rajshahi, Chattogram, Cumilla, Jashore, Sylhet, Barishal, Dinajpur, Mymensingh), the Madrasah Education Board (Dakhil), and the Technical Education Board (Vocational).

The result website is not loading. What should I do?

On result day almost two million students visit at once, so the official servers slow down. Wait a few minutes and refresh with Ctrl + R, or use the SMS method to 16222, which does not depend on the website. Avoid entering your details on unofficial mirror sites.

How do I apply for re-scrutiny, and does it help?

After results, apply by SMS from a Teletalk SIM (format beginning with RSC) within the application window, paying roughly 125–150 Taka per subject. Re-scrutiny re-checks whether marks were added and recorded correctly rather than re-marking the paper, so apply only for subjects where you have a genuine reason to doubt the result.

Is this an official education board website?

No. This is an independent information website that explains the result process. Official results are published only by the education boards, through educationboardresults.gov.bd and eboardresults.com. If anything here ever differs from an official notice, the board's notice is the correct one.
