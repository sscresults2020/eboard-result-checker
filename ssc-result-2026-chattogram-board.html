The result was first scheduled for 20 July 2026, then postponed; the boards have confirmed publication on 10 August 2026, released at 2:00 PM alongside every other board. SECTION 02How to Check SSC Result 2026 Chattogram Board Online

Checking online reveals the full marksheet with a grade for every subject. Have your admit card (roll) and registration card (registration number) ready first.

  1. Open an official portal. Use eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or the board site bise-ctg.gov.bd.
  2. Select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Chattogram as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet at once with a screenshot or print.

If the website lags, do not worry. Heavy traffic at 2:00 PM slows the servers for everyone. Refresh after a short wait, or use the SMS method below, which keeps working regardless.

SECTION 03Check Chattogram Board Result 2026 by SMS

SMS is the reliable backup when the site is busy. Send this to 16222:

SSC CHI <ROLL> 2026

Swap <ROLL> for your roll number. For roll 123456 you would send SSC CHI 123456 2026 to 16222 and get your GPA by return message. A small charge applies per SMS. Prepare the message before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts for the moment results are released.

SECTION 04Chattogram Board SSC Result 2025: The Numbers

Last year sets your baseline for 2026. In 2025, Chattogram recorded a pass rate of 72.07 percent, placing it third among the general boards that year. Around 1,40,388 students appeared, 1,01,181 passed, and 11,843 earned GPA 5. Girls again outperformed boys: 6,353 female students secured GPA 5 against 5,490 male students, and more girls sat and passed overall.

SECTION 06Chattogram Board Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

For the subject-wise marksheet with number and grade, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only your GPA. The result page lists every subject with its grade and grade point plus your final GPA. The online copy is provisional; the printed grade sheet and certificate follow through your school, but the online version is accepted for class XI admission, so you can begin applying without delay.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

When your Chattogram Board marksheet loads, the figure everyone looks at first is the GPA, scored on a scale from 1.00 to 5.00. The top grade, GPA 5.00, is what students call A+. Each subject carries a letter grade and a matching grade point, and your GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted in a special way: points above a grade C in the 4th subject are added to your total, which is how many students lift their GPA to a full 5.00. To pass overall you must earn at least a grade D, meaning grade point 1.00, in every compulsory and elective subject. A single F pulls the whole subject into fail, so read every row of the marksheet, not just the final number.

SECTION 07If Your Chattogram Result Looks Wrong

Should a subject look lower than expected, apply for re-scrutiny (board challenge). It re-checks the marking mechanics, whether every answer was marked and the totals added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking the script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply selectively, for subjects you genuinely doubt.

SECTION 08After the Result: College Admission

For most Chattogram Board students, the result quickly turns into a bigger question: which college next? Class XI admission runs through a central online system that opens soon after results, and because this year's result was delayed, the gap before admission is tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your group and subject grades, so your realistic targets follow directly from the marksheet you have just checked.

A practical approach is to build a shortlist in three tiers: a few ambitious colleges such as the top colleges of Chattogram city, several realistic choices, and a couple of safe options you would still be content with. Keep your grade sheet saved as a file, know your exact GPA, and be ready to apply the moment the admission circular appears. Treat result day and admission as one connected process rather than two separate events, and you will not lose momentum when the window opens.
