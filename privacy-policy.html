Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control.

Last updated · 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Exam Results BD collects when you visit examresultsbd.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to what is described here.

The short version. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your result details. We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, and we show advertising that may use cookies. You can switch off ad personalisation and clear cookies at any time.

1. Who we are

Exam Results BD is an independent information website about SSC and equivalent examination results in Bangladesh. For anything relating to privacy, write to contact@examresultsbd.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

If you email us or use the form on our Contact page, we receive whatever you choose to write, typically your name, your email address and your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix whatever you have reported.

We ask you not to send roll numbers, registration numbers, national identity details or copies of documents. We do not need them and we do not want to hold them.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, our host and our analytics tools record basic technical information when a page is loaded:

  • IP address (used in a shortened or anonymised form by our analytics provider)
  • Browser type and version, and device type
  • Operating system and screen size
  • The page you visited, when you visited, and how long you stayed
  • The website or search engine that sent you to us
  • Approximate location at city or country level, derived from your IP address

This is statistical information. We use it to see which pages are useful, when traffic peaks, and whether the site works properly on the devices people actually use. We do not use it to identify you personally.

2.3 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect examination results, roll numbers or registration numbers through this website.
  • We do not ask for passwords, payment details or financial information.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children (see section 8).
  • We do not sell personal information to anyone.

3. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. We and our partners use them for analytics and advertising. A full explanation of each type, and how to control or remove them, is in our Cookie Policy.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes the technical information listed in section 2.2. The reports we see are aggregated, visitor counts, popular pages, devices, and general locations, not profiles of individuals.

If you would rather not be included, Google publishes a browser add-on that opts you out of Google Analytics on every site you visit: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

5. Advertising

This website is free, and advertising is what pays for it. Advertisements may be served by third-party advertising networks, including Google and its partners.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on your previous visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to you based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices and optout.networkadvertising.org.

Turning off personalisation does not remove advertising; it means the advertisements you see are less closely matched to your interests. We do not control what any third-party network collects, and their own privacy policies apply to their activity.

7. Sharing information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Information is shared only with:

  • Service providers that make the site work, our hosting and content-delivery provider, our analytics provider and our advertising partners, and only to the extent needed for those services.
  • Authorities, where we are required to do so by law or by a valid legal request.

8. Children

Many people reading about SSC results are under 18. We have designed this site so that no account, registration or personal detail is needed to read anything on it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to contact@examresultsbd.com and we will delete it.

9. How long we keep information

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to deal with the matter and to keep a record of corrections. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics provider's standard retention settings, in aggregated form.

10. Your choices

  • Cookies, block or delete them in your browser settings, as described in our Cookie Policy.
  • Analytics, opt out using the Google browser add-on linked in section 4.
  • Advertising, turn off personalisation using the links in section 5.
  • Email, ask us to delete the correspondence you have sent us.
  • Do Not Track, browsers vary in how they send this signal and there is no agreed standard for responding to it, so we do not rely on it. Use the controls above instead.

11. Links to other websites

Our pages link to official education board portals and occasionally to news reports. Once you follow a link you are on someone else's website, governed by their privacy policy, not ours. Please read theirs before giving them information.

12. Security

The site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. No website can promise perfect security, but because we deliberately collect so little, there is very little about you for us to lose.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes or as our advertising and analytics partners change their practices. The date at the top of this page shows the current version. Significant changes will be noted on the site.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request relating to your information, can be sent to contact@examresultsbd.com with the subject line Privacy request.
