SSC Result 2026 with Marksheet

How to check your result with the full subject-wise marksheet, the grade and number in every subject, using your roll and registration number.

Updated 28 July 2026

When results come out, most students want more than a single GPA figure. They want the full marksheet, the sheet that shows the grade and the number in every subject. That detail is what tells you where you did well, where a subject pulled your average down, and exactly what colleges will look at during admission. This guide explains how to check the SSC Result 2026 with marksheet the right way, why some students see only a GPA, and how to save the sheet properly once it loads.

SECTION 01What the SSC Marksheet Actually Shows

The marksheet, sometimes called the grade sheet, is the detailed version of your result. Instead of a single number, it lists every compulsory and elective subject with the letter grade and the grade point you earned in each. It also shows your overall GPA and, importantly, how the optional 4th subject affected it. For a student comparing options for college, this per subject view matters far more than the headline GPA, because two students with the same GPA can have very different strengths across subjects.

There is one thing to keep clear from the start. To see this full marksheet, you must check online and enter both your roll and registration number. The SMS method, useful as it is on a busy day, returns only your GPA and whether you passed. If you want the number in every subject, the online portal is the only route.

SECTION 02How to Check SSC Result 2026 with Marksheet Online

Before you start, keep two documents within reach: your admit card, which carries the six digit roll number, and your registration card, which carries the ten digit registration number. Both are needed to unlock the complete grade sheet. Then follow these steps.

  1. Open an official portal. Go to eboardresults.com or educationboardresults.gov.bd. You can also use the checker on our homepage, which opens the official portal for you.
  2. Select the examination. Choose SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent from the exam list.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select your board. Pick the board printed on your admit card, such as Dhaka, Rajshahi or Chattogram.
  5. Enter your roll number and registration number. This is the key step. Entering both, not just the roll, is what shows the full marksheet instead of only the GPA.
  6. Answer the security check. Solve the simple sum shown to prove you are a real visitor.
  7. Press Get Result. Your subject-wise marksheet appears on screen. Save it straight away.

The single most common mistake: entering only the roll number. Many students do this, see just a GPA, and think the marksheet is unavailable. Add your registration number and the full subject-wise sheet appears.

SECTION 03Why You Might See Only a GPA

If your result shows a GPA but no subject breakdown, there is almost always a simple reason. The most frequent is the one above, checking with the roll number alone. The second is that you used the SMS method, which is designed to be quick and light and therefore returns only the GPA and pass status. The third, on result day itself, is heavy traffic: when the portal is overloaded it sometimes returns a simplified view, and refreshing after a few minutes brings back the full sheet.

So if the number in each subject is what you are after, check online, enter both numbers, and if the site is slow at 2:00 PM, wait a little and try again rather than assuming the detailed marksheet is missing.

SECTION 04Reading Your Marksheet: Grades and GPA

Each subject on the marksheet carries a letter grade and a matching grade point. The scale is the same across every board.

MarksLetter gradeGrade point
80 to 100A+5.00
70 to 79A4.00
60 to 69A minus3.50
50 to 59B3.00
40 to 49C2.00
33 to 39D1.00
0 to 32F0.00

Your overall GPA is the average of the grade points across your main subjects, with the 4th subject handled specially: points above a grade C in the 4th subject are added to your total as a bonus, which is how many students reach a full 5.00. An F in any main subject means the overall result is a fail, regardless of the other grades. If you want to estimate your GPA before the result, try our SSC GPA calculator, which applies these rules for you.

SECTION 05How to Save and Print Your Marksheet

Once your marksheet is on screen, save it immediately, because result-day servers are unpredictable and you do not want to be hunting for it again an hour later. On a phone, a screenshot is fastest. On a computer, use your browser's print option and choose Save as PDF. Keep a copy in your email or cloud storage as well, since you will be showing or uploading this grade sheet repeatedly during college admission.

Grade sheet versus certificate. The marksheet you see online right after results is the provisional grade sheet. The original printed grade sheet and certificate come later through your school. For Class XI admission, the online grade sheet is accepted, so you can begin applying straight away.

SECTION 07What the Numbers Looked Like in 2025

Reading last year's national picture helps set expectations before you open your own marksheet. In 2025 the combined pass rate across all boards fell to 68.45 percent, the lowest in about 16 years, with 139,032 students earning GPA 5. The chart below shows the five year trend.

The lesson for 2026 is simple. When pass rates fall, marking has usually been stricter, and a strong marksheet is worth more in the admission race that follows. Whatever your own numbers are, read the full sheet rather than the GPA alone, because the subject detail is what shapes your next step.

SECTION 08If a Subject Number Looks Wrong

If a subject on your marksheet comes back lower than you were certain of, you can apply for re-scrutiny, often called a board challenge. It does not re-mark your paper from scratch. Instead it re-checks the mechanics, whether every answer was marked, whether the marks were added correctly, and whether the total was carried over properly. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a real reason, since most applications do not change a grade.

SECTION 09Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my SSC Result 2026 with marksheet?
Open eboardresults.com, select SSC, year 2026 and your board, then enter both your roll number and registration number. Entering both is what shows the full subject-wise marksheet rather than only the GPA.
Why does my SSC result show only GPA and not the marksheet?
If you see only the GPA, you most likely entered the roll number without the registration number, or you checked by SMS. The SMS reply only returns the GPA. To see the number in every subject, use the online portal and enter both roll and registration.
Can I get the SSC marksheet with number by SMS?
No. The SMS service to 16222 returns your GPA and pass status only. The subject-wise marksheet with numbers is available only through the online portals such as eboardresults.com.
Is the online marksheet the same as the original certificate?
The marksheet you see online right after results is the provisional grade sheet. It is accepted for Class XI admission, so you can apply without waiting. The original printed grade sheet and certificate are distributed later through your school.
