SECTION 01Mymensingh Education Board: An Overview

The Mymensingh Education Board, established in 2017, is the newest education board in Bangladesh, created to serve the greater Mymensingh region covering the districts of Mymensingh, Jamalpur, Netrokona and Sherpur. Before its formation, students here sat under the Dhaka Board; the dedicated board now serves this populous north-central region directly.

Mymensingh city is a well-established education centre, home to respected institutions and the nearby Bangladesh Agricultural University. As a newer board, Mymensingh is still building its track record, but the region has a strong academic tradition. For a Mymensingh Board student, the SSC result opens up college admission across the division and keeps options open for competitive colleges in nearby Dhaka.

SECTION 02When Will the Mymensingh Board SSC Result 2026 Be Published?

Here is the answer everyone opens this page for. The result was first scheduled for 20 July 2026, then postponed, and the boards have now confirmed publication on 10 August 2026. Mymensingh releases at the same moment as every board, around 2:00 PM. The date was fixed through the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee and reported by Prothom Alo and Dhaka Tribune. A delay does not change your marks, so trust official Mymensingh Board notices over the guesses that spread every year.

SECTION 03How to Check SSC Result 2026 Mymensingh Board Online

The online method shows the full marksheet with a grade in every subject. Keep your admit card (six digit roll) and registration card (ten digit registration) ready before you start.

  1. Open an official portal. Go to eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or mymensingheducationboard.gov.bd.
  2. Select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Mymensingh as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet straight away.

If the site slows down, stay calm. Close to two million students refresh at once when results drop, so a delay is normal and does not mean your result is missing. Wait and refresh, or use the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website.

SECTION 04Check Mymensingh Board Result 2026 by SMS

SMS works on any handset with no internet, and it shines when the website is busiest. Send this to 16222:

SSC MYM <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your roll number. A Mymensingh student with roll 123456 would send SSC MYM 123456 2026 to 16222 and receive the GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Type it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts.

SECTION 05Mymensingh Board SSC Result 2025: The Numbers

Last year is the clearest guide for 2026. In 2025, Mymensingh recorded a pass rate of 58.22 percent, the second lowest among the general boards, ahead of only Barishal. Out of 1,07,161 students who appeared, 6,678 earned GPA 5. As the country's youngest general board, Mymensingh serves a smaller candidate pool than the older boards, and 2025 was a difficult year across its four districts.

SECTION 07Mymensingh Board Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

For the subject-wise marksheet with grade and number, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page then lists each subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your overall GPA. Read the whole sheet, not just the final number, because it shows exactly where a subject helped or hurt your average and it is the same sheet colleges use during admission. For the complete method, see our detailed guide on how to get the Mymensingh Board result with marksheet.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

Your GPA runs from 1.00 to 5.00, and the top grade, GPA 5.00, is what students call A+. Each subject earns a letter grade and a matching grade point, and the GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted so that points above a grade C are added to your total, which is how many students reach a full 5.00. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, grade point 1.00, in every subject, because a single F fails that subject.

SECTION 08Common Mistakes to Avoid on Result Day

Four avoidable errors catch Mymensingh students every year. First, do not confuse your roll and registration numbers; the full marksheet needs the correct registration, so check your card first. Second, avoid unofficial mirror sites that promise a faster result, since they may show wrong data or misuse your details. Third, do not panic when the site stalls at release; that is server load, and SMS to 16222 avoids it. Fourth, save the marksheet the moment it loads, because you will need it for admission and the servers may slow again later.

SECTION 09If Your Mymensingh Result Looks Wrong

If a subject comes back lower than you expected, apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. For the full process, fees and dates, see our guide on how to apply for the SSC board challenge. It re-checks whether every answer was marked and the marks added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a genuine reason, since most applications do not change a grade.

SECTION 10After the Result: College Admission

For most Mymensingh Board students, the result quickly becomes a question of which college? Class XI admission runs through a central online system that opens soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your subject grades. Build a shortlist in three tiers, a few ambitious names such as the leading colleges of Mymensingh city, several realistic choices, and a couple of safe options, and apply the moment the circular appears.
