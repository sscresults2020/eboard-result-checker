SECTION 01When Will the Jashore Board SSC Result 2026 Be Published?

The result was first set for 20 July 2026, then postponed; the boards have confirmed publication on 10 August 2026, released at 2:00 PM with every board. A delay does not affect your marks. Trust only official Jashore Board notices rather than unofficial dates, and check back here as soon as a firm date is set.

Please note: the SSC Result 2026 date of 10 August 2026 has been confirmed by the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee, reported by Prothom Alo and Dhaka Tribune on 27 July 2026. We update this page if any official change is announced, so follow the announcement bar on our homepage.

SECTION 02How to Check SSC Result 2026 Jashore Board Online

Checking online reveals the full marksheet with a grade for every subject. Have your admit card (roll) and registration card ready.

  1. Open an official portal. Use eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or jessoreboard.gov.bd.
  2. Select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Jashore as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet at once.

If the website lags, do not worry. Millions check at 2:00 PM and the servers strain. Refresh after a short wait, or use SMS below, which keeps working regardless.

SECTION 03Check Jashore Board Result 2026 by SMS

SMS is the reliable backup when the site is busy. Send this to 16222:

SSC JES <ROLL> 2026

Swap <ROLL> for your roll number. For roll 123456 you would send SSC JES 123456 2026 to 16222 and receive your GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Prepare it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts.

SECTION 06Jashore Board Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

For the subject-wise marksheet with number and grade, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page then lists each subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your overall GPA.

Beyond the headline GPA, the marksheet is worth reading in full. It shows the grade you earned in every subject, which tells you where you were strong and where a subject dragged the average down. That detail matters for the group and college you target next, and it is the same sheet colleges look at during admission, so take a moment to understand every line rather than glancing only at the final number.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

The number everyone looks at first is the GPA, scored from 1.00 to 5.00, where the top grade of GPA 5.00 is what students call A+. Each subject carries a letter grade and a matching grade point, and your GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted specially: points above a grade C in the 4th subject are added to your total, which is how many students reach a full 5.00. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, meaning grade point 1.00, in every compulsory and elective subject. One F pulls that subject into fail, so read every row, not just the final figure.

SECTION 07If Your Jashore Result Looks Wrong

Should a subject look lower than you expected, you may apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. It re-checks the marking mechanics, whether every answer was marked and the marks added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only for subjects you genuinely doubt, since most applications do not change a grade.

Common mistakes to avoid on result day

A few simple errors trip up Jashore Board students every year, and all of them are easy to sidestep. First, entering the wrong registration number: the roll and registration are different numbers, and the full marksheet will not open without the correct registration, so check your registration card before you start. Second, trusting unofficial mirror sites that promise a faster result; they may show wrong data or misuse the roll and registration you type in, so stay on the official portals or a trusted checker. Third, giving up when the site stalls at 2:00 PM; that is congestion, not a missing result, and the SMS route to 16222 sidesteps it entirely. Finally, forgetting to save the marksheet once it loads; take a screenshot and use the print option immediately, because you will need it for college admission and the servers may slow again later in the day.

SECTION 08After the Result: College Admission

For most Jashore Board students, the result quickly becomes a question of which college next? Class XI admission runs through a central online system that opens soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your group and subject grades. Build a shortlist in three tiers, a few ambitious names such as the leading colleges of Khulna and Jashore, several realistic choices, and a couple of safe options, and be ready to apply the moment the circular appears.
