SECTION 01Bangladesh Technical Education Board: An Overview

The Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB) oversees the SSC (Vocational) examination and technical education across the whole country, rather than a single region. Established in 1954 and reorganised over the years, it administers vocational and technical curricula designed to build practical, job-ready skills alongside academic subjects, and examines SSC Vocational candidates from technical schools and institutes nationwide.

Its candidates come from every part of Bangladesh, drawn to the hands-on, skills-focused pathway that technical education offers. An SSC Vocational result is equivalent to general SSC for further study, so a strong result opens up admission to HSC Vocational, polytechnic diplomas, and general college alike. For an SSC Vocational student, the result is the first step toward a technical or academic career path.

SECTION 02When Will the SSC Vocational Result 2026 Be Published?

Here is the short answer. The result was first scheduled for 20 July 2026, then postponed, and the boards have now confirmed publication on 10 August 2026. The Technical Board releases its SSC Vocational result at the same moment as every general board, around 2:00 PM. The date was fixed through the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee and reported by Prothom Alo and Dhaka Tribune. A delay does not change your marks, so rely on official Technical Board notices over the unofficial dates that spread each year.

Please note: the SSC and SSC Vocational Result 2026 date of 10 August 2026 has been confirmed by the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee. We update this page if anything official changes, so follow the announcement bar on our homepage.

SECTION 03How to Check SSC Vocational Result 2026 Online

Checking online shows the full marksheet with a grade in every subject. Keep your admit card (roll number) and registration card (registration number) beside you before you start.

  1. Open an official portal. Go to eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or the board site bteb.gov.bd.
  2. Select the examination. Choose SSC Vocational (listed under SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent).
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Technical as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet at once with a screenshot or print.

If the site slows down, stay calm. Close to two million students refresh at once when results drop, so a delay is normal. Wait and refresh, or use the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website.

SECTION 04Check SSC Vocational Result 2026 by SMS

SMS works on any handset with no internet, and it is dependable when the website is busiest. Send this to 16222:

SSC TEC <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your roll number. A Technical student with roll 123456 would send SSC TEC 123456 2026 to 16222 and receive the GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Type it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts, ready to send the moment results go live.

SECTION 05SSC Vocational Result 2025: The Numbers

Last year sets your baseline for 2026. In 2025, the Technical Board recorded an SSC Vocational pass rate of 73.63 percent, one of the stronger results among all boards that year. Out of about 1,38,204 students who appeared, 4,948 earned GPA 5. The Technical Board held up notably well in a year when most general boards fell sharply, finishing above the national average of 68.45 percent.

SECTION 07SSC Vocational Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

To view the marksheet with grade and number for each subject, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page lists every subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your overall GPA. Read it in full, because the sheet shows where you were strong and is the same one polytechnics and colleges review during admission. For the full step-by-step method, see our guide on checking the SSC Vocational result with marksheet.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

The SSC Vocational GPA uses the same 1.00 to 5.00 scale as the general SSC, and the top grade, GPA 5.00, is A+. Each subject earns a letter grade and a matching grade point, and the GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted so that points above a grade C are added to your total. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, grade point 1.00, in every subject, because a single F fails that subject.

SECTION 08Common Mistakes to Avoid on Result Day

A few avoidable errors catch Technical students every year. First, do not confuse your roll and registration numbers; the full marksheet needs the correct registration, so check your card first. Second, avoid unofficial mirror sites that promise a faster result, since they may show wrong data or misuse your details. Third, do not panic when the site stalls at release; that is server load, and SMS to 16222 avoids it. Fourth, save the marksheet the moment it loads, because you will need it for diploma or college admission and the servers may slow again later.

SECTION 09If Your SSC Vocational Result Looks Wrong

If a subject comes back lower than you expected, you may apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. For the complete re-scrutiny process and fees, read our guide on how to apply for the SSC board challenge. It re-checks whether every answer was marked and the marks added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a genuine reason, since most applications do not change a grade.

SECTION 10After the Result: Diploma and College Options

For most SSC Vocational students, the result quickly becomes a question of what next? You can continue on the technical path with a diploma in engineering or a related field at a polytechnic institute, or move into HSC Vocational or a general college for Class XI. Admission opens soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your GPA, so your realistic options follow from the marksheet you have just checked. Decide early which path suits your goals and keep your grade sheet saved and ready.
