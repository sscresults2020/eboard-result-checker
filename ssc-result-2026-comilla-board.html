SECTION 01When Will the Comilla Board SSC Result 2026 Come Out?

The result was first set for 20 July 2026, then postponed; the boards now expect the last week of July, released at 2:00 PM alongside every board. A short delay does not change your marks, so rely only on official Cumilla Board notices rather than the unofficial dates that spread each year. We update this page as soon as a firm date is announced.

Please note: the Ministry of Education has not yet fixed the final date in writing. Treat "last week of July" as a strong expectation and watch the announcement bar on our homepage.

SECTION 02How to Check SSC Result 2026 Comilla Board Online

The online method shows the full marksheet with a grade in every subject. Keep your admit card (roll) and registration card ready first.

  1. Open an official portal. Use eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or comillaboard.gov.bd.
  2. Select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Comilla as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save the marksheet straight away with a screenshot or print.

If the site is slow, stay calm. Heavy traffic at 2:00 PM slows the servers for everyone. Refresh after a moment, or use the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website.

SECTION 03Check Comilla Board Result 2026 by SMS

SMS works on any phone with no internet. Send this to 16222:

SSC COM <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your roll number. For roll 123456 you would send SSC COM 123456 2026 to 16222 and get your GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Prepare it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts.

SECTION 06Cumilla Board Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

For the subject-wise marksheet with number and grade, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page then lists each subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your overall GPA.

Beyond the headline GPA, the marksheet is worth reading in full. It shows the grade you earned in every subject, which tells you where you were strong and where a subject dragged the average down. That detail matters for the group and college you target next, and it is the same sheet colleges look at during admission, so take a moment to understand every line rather than glancing only at the final number.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

The number everyone looks at first is the GPA, scored from 1.00 to 5.00, where the top grade of GPA 5.00 is what students call A+. Each subject carries a letter grade and a matching grade point, and your GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted specially: points above a grade C in the 4th subject are added to your total, which is how many students reach a full 5.00. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, meaning grade point 1.00, in every compulsory and elective subject. One F pulls that subject into fail, so read every row, not just the final figure.

SECTION 07If Your Cumilla Result Looks Wrong

Should a subject look lower than you expected, you may apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. It re-checks the marking mechanics, whether every answer was marked and the marks added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only for subjects you genuinely doubt, since most applications do not change a grade.

Common mistakes to avoid on result day

A few simple errors trip up Cumilla Board students every year, and all of them are easy to sidestep. First, entering the wrong registration number: the roll and registration are different numbers, and the full marksheet will not open without the correct registration, so check your registration card before you start. Second, trusting unofficial mirror sites that promise a faster result; they may show wrong data or misuse the roll and registration you type in, so stay on the official portals or a trusted checker. Third, giving up when the site stalls at 2:00 PM; that is congestion, not a missing result, and the SMS route to 16222 sidesteps it entirely. Finally, forgetting to save the marksheet once it loads; take a screenshot and use the print option immediately, because you will need it for college admission and the servers may slow again later in the day.

SECTION 08After the Result: College Admission

For most Cumilla Board students, the result quickly becomes a question of which college next? Class XI admission runs through a central online system that opens soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your group and subject grades. Build a shortlist in three tiers, a few ambitious names such as the top colleges of Cumilla and Chandpur, several realistic choices, and a couple of safe options, and be ready to apply the moment the circular appears.
