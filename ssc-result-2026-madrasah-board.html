SECTION 01When Will the Dakhil Result 2026 Be Published?

Here is the short answer. The result was first scheduled for 20 July 2026, then postponed, and the boards have now confirmed publication on 10 August 2026. The Madrasah Board releases its Dakhil result at the same moment as every general board, around 2:00 PM. The date was fixed through the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee and reported by Prothom Alo and Dhaka Tribune. A delay does not change your marks, so rely on official Madrasah Board notices over the unofficial dates that circulate each year.

Please note: the Dakhil and SSC Result 2026 date of 10 August 2026 has been confirmed by the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee. We update this page if anything official changes, so follow the announcement bar on our homepage.

SECTION 02How to Check Dakhil Result 2026 Online

Checking online shows the full marksheet with a grade in every subject. Keep your admit card (roll number) and registration card (registration number) beside you before you start.

  1. Open an official portal. Go to eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or the board site bmeb.gov.bd.
  2. Select the examination. Choose Dakhil (listed under SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent).
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Madrasah as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet at once with a screenshot or print.

If the site slows down, stay calm. Close to two million SSC and Dakhil students refresh at once when results drop, so a delay is normal. Wait and refresh, or use the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website.

SECTION 03Check Dakhil Result 2026 by SMS

For Dakhil, the SMS format differs slightly from the general boards: the message begins with DAKHIL rather than SSC. Send this to 16222:

DAKHIL MAD <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your roll number. A Dakhil student with roll 123456 would send DAKHIL MAD 123456 2026 to 16222 and receive the GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Type it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts, ready to send the moment results go live.

SECTION 04Dakhil Result 2025: The Numbers

Last year sets your baseline for 2026. In 2025, the Madrasah Board recorded a Dakhil pass rate of 68.09 percent. Out of 2,86,572 students who appeared, 1,95,115 passed, and 9,066 earned GPA 5. The Madrasah Board is one of the largest by candidate numbers, so while its GPA 5 rate is modest at roughly 3 percent of candidates, the absolute number of Dakhil students it serves is very high.

SECTION 06Dakhil Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

To view the marksheet with grade and number for each subject, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page lists every subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your overall GPA. Read it in full, because the sheet shows where you were strong and is the same one madrasahs and colleges review during Alim and college admission.

Understanding your Dakhil GPA

The Dakhil GPA uses the same 1.00 to 5.00 scale as the SSC, and the top grade, GPA 5.00, is A+. Each subject earns a letter grade and a matching grade point, and the GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted so that points above a grade C are added to your total. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, grade point 1.00, in every subject, because a single F fails that subject.

SECTION 07Common Mistakes to Avoid on Result Day

A few avoidable errors catch Dakhil students every year. First, do not confuse your roll and registration numbers; the full marksheet needs the correct registration, so check your card first. Second, remember the SMS begins with DAKHIL, not SSC, or the message will not return your result. Third, avoid unofficial mirror sites that promise a faster result, since they may show wrong data or misuse your details. Fourth, save the marksheet the moment it loads, because you will need it for Alim or college admission and the servers may slow again later.

SECTION 08If Your Dakhil Result Looks Wrong

If a subject comes back lower than you expected, you may apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. It re-checks whether every answer was marked and the marks added and carried correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a genuine reason, since most applications do not change a grade.

SECTION 09After Dakhil: Alim and College Admission

For most Dakhil students, the result quickly becomes a question of what next? You can continue in the madrasah stream by enrolling in Alim, the madrasah equivalent of HSC, or move into a general college for Class XI. Both routes open soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your Dakhil GPA, so your realistic options follow from the marksheet you have just checked. Decide early which stream suits your goals and keep your grade sheet saved and ready.
