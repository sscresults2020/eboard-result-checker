SECTION 01Dinajpur Education Board: An Overview

The Dinajpur Education Board, established in 2006, is one of the newer boards and serves the far north of Bangladesh, covering districts including Dinajpur, Rangpur, Thakurgaon, Panchagarh, Nilphamari, Lalmonirhat, Kurigram and Gaibandha. Carved out to serve the northern region more directly, it examines a large candidate base spread across the country's northernmost districts.

The region has steadily strengthened its results since the board was formed, and Dinajpur and Rangpur cities serve as its main education centres. For a Dinajpur Board student, the SSC result is the gateway to college admission across the north, and a strong GPA is valued when competing for seats at the region's better colleges and beyond.

SECTION 02When Will the Dinajpur Board SSC Result 2026 Be Published?

Here is the short answer. The result was first set for 20 July 2026, then postponed, and the boards have now confirmed publication on 10 August 2026. Dinajpur releases at the same time as every other board, around 2:00 PM. The date was fixed through the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee and reported by Prothom Alo and Dhaka Tribune. A delay changes nothing about your marks, so rely on official Dinajpur Board notices rather than the unofficial dates that circulate each year.

Please note: the SSC Result 2026 date of 10 August 2026 has been confirmed by the Inter-Education Board Coordination Committee. We update this page if anything official changes, so follow the announcement bar on our homepage.

SECTION 03How to Check SSC Result 2026 Dinajpur Board Online

Checking online shows the full marksheet with a grade in every subject. Keep your admit card (six digit roll) and registration card (ten digit registration) beside you before you start.

  1. Open an official portal. Go to eboardresults.com, educationboardresults.gov.bd, or dinajpurboard.gov.bd.
  2. Select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Dinajpur as your board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number.
  6. Answer the security check and press Get Result.
  7. Save your marksheet at once with a screenshot or print.

If the site slows down, stay calm. Nearly two million students refresh at once when results drop, so a delay is normal. Wait a little and refresh, or use the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website.

SECTION 04Check Dinajpur Board Result 2026 by SMS

SMS is the reliable backup when the website is busiest. Send this to 16222:

SSC DIN <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your roll number. A Dinajpur student with roll 123456 would send SSC DIN 123456 2026 to 16222 and get the GPA by return message. A small charge applies. Type it before 2:00 PM and keep it in drafts, ready to send the moment results go live.

SECTION 05Dinajpur Board SSC Result 2025: The Numbers

Last year sets your baseline for 2026. In 2025, Dinajpur recorded a pass rate of 67.03 percent. Out of 1,82,410 students who appeared, 1,22,146 passed, and 15,062 earned GPA 5. That GPA 5 tally was the fourth highest of any board in the country, behind only Dhaka, Rajshahi and Jashore, which is notable for a northern board. Female students edged ahead, with 7,546 girls securing GPA 5 to 7,516 boys.

SECTION 07Dinajpur Board Result 2026 with Full Marksheet

To view the marksheet with the grade and number for each subject, use the online method and enter both your roll and registration number, since SMS returns only the GPA. The result page lists every subject with its grade and grade point, followed by your final GPA. Read it in full rather than glancing only at the last figure, because the sheet shows where you were strong and is the same one colleges review during admission. For step-by-step instructions, read our dedicated guide on checking the Dinajpur Board result with marksheet.

Understanding your GPA on the marksheet

Your GPA runs from 1.00 to 5.00, and the top grade, GPA 5.00, is the one students call A+. Each subject earns a letter grade and a matching grade point, and the GPA is the average across them, with the optional 4th subject adjusted specially: points above a grade C in the 4th subject are added to your total, which is how many students reach a full 5.00. To pass overall you need at least a grade D, grade point 1.00, in every subject, because a single F fails that subject.

SECTION 08Common Mistakes to Avoid on Result Day

A few avoidable slips catch Dinajpur students every year. First, do not confuse your roll and registration numbers; the marksheet will not open in full without the correct registration, so check your registration card first. Second, avoid unofficial mirror sites promising a faster result, as they may show wrong data or misuse what you type in. Third, do not panic when the site stalls at release; that is server load, and the SMS route to 16222 avoids it. Fourth, save the marksheet the moment it appears, because you will need it for admission and the servers may slow again later.

SECTION 09If Your Dinajpur Result Looks Wrong

If a subject comes back lower than you expected, you may apply for re-scrutiny, the board challenge. For the full re-scrutiny process and deadline, read our guide on applying for the SSC board challenge. It re-checks whether every answer was marked and whether the marks were added and carried over correctly, rather than re-marking your script. Applications open the day after results for about a week, are made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM in a format beginning with RSC, and cost roughly 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a genuine reason, since most applications do not change a grade.

SECTION 10After the Result: College Admission

For most Dinajpur Board students, the result soon becomes a question of which college? Class XI admission runs through a central online system that opens soon after results, and this year's delay makes the gap tighter than usual. Admission is largely merit based on your SSC GPA and, for many colleges, your subject grades, so your realistic targets follow from the marksheet you have just checked. Build a shortlist in three tiers, a few ambitious names such as the leading colleges of Rangpur and Dinajpur, several realistic choices, and a couple of safe options, and be ready when the circular appears.
