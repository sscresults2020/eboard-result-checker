Cookie Policy

Which cookies this website uses, and exactly how to switch them off.

Last updated · 25 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which ones are used on examresultsbd.com, and exactly how to control or remove them. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. When you come back, the browser sends the file back, so the site can recognise the browser and remember simple things. Cookies cannot read your files, run programs or carry viruses.

Cookies are either first-party (set by the site you are on) or third-party (set by another company whose content appears on the page, such as an advertising network). They are also either session cookies, deleted when you close the browser, or persistent cookies, which stay for a set period.

2. Cookies used on this site

2.1 Strictly necessary

A very small number of cookies are needed for the site to load correctly and securely through our hosting and content-delivery provider. They keep the site available and help block malicious traffic. Without them the site would not work reliably, so they cannot be switched off from within the site, though you can still block them in your browser.

2.2 Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visitors and see which pages are read. These cookies tell us how many people came, which guides were most useful, and whether pages worked on a given device. They do not tell us who you are.

2.3 Advertising

Advertisements on this site may be served by third-party networks, including Google and its partners. These networks may set cookies to limit how often you see the same advertisement, to measure whether an advertisement worked, and to show advertising related to your interests based on your visits to this and other websites.

4. Turning off personalised advertising

  • Google Ads Settings, switch off ad personalisation on Google services.
  • aboutads.info/choices, opt out across participating networks.
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out
  • Google Analytics opt-out add-on
Opting out does not remove advertisements. It only means they are chosen without using your browsing history, so they will be less relevant to you. Advertising is what keeps this site free to read.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you see the cookies stored, delete them, and block future ones. The settings are usually under Privacy or Site settings:

  • Chrome (Android and desktop), Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies / Delete browsing data
  • Safari (iPhone and Mac), Settings › Safari › Block All Cookies, or Clear History and Website Data
  • Firefox, Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge, Settings › Cookies and site permissions
  • Opera, Settings › Privacy & security › Cookies

Most browsers also offer a private or incognito window, which discards cookies when you close it.

If you block everything. The pages of this site will still be readable, because nothing here depends on you being recognised. Blocking cookies mainly affects advertising and our visitor statistics.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the cookies used on the site change. The date at the top shows the current version.
