SECTION 01Dhaka Education Board: An Overview

The Dhaka Education Board, established in 1962, is the largest and arguably the most influential of the eleven education boards in Bangladesh. It oversees secondary and higher secondary education across the greater Dhaka region, and because the capital concentrates the country's population, institutions and resources, the board consistently registers the highest number of candidates and the highest number of GPA 5 achievers in the country. In 2025 alone, more than 3,79,000 students sat the SSC under this board, a figure larger than several boards combined.

What sets the Dhaka Board apart is not only its size but the density of strong institutions within its area. The capital is home to many of the nation's most competitive schools and colleges, a large share of English-medium and cadet-standard institutions, and the widest range of coaching and academic support. For a serious student, being under the Dhaka Board means sitting alongside some of the most competitive peers in Bangladesh, which raises both the challenge and the value of a strong result.

SECTION 02When Will the Dhaka Board SSC Result 2026 Be Published?

The Dhaka Board SSC Result 2026 will be published on 10 August 2026, at around 2:00 PM, at the same time as every other education board. You can check it online at eboardresults.com or by SMS to 16222 using your roll and registration number.

For context: the results were originally scheduled for 20 July 2026, but that date was postponed and the boards have confirmed the new date of 10 August 2026. Because the Dhaka Board sits at the centre of the inter board coordination committee, its result is released at the same moment as every other board on the announced day.

Although a delay is frustrating when you have been waiting for weeks, it does not change your marks in any way. What it does change is which "date" you should trust. Every year, unofficial pages circulate confident dates that turn out to be guesses, so please rely on official Dhaka Board notices and reputable news outlets. We update this page the moment a firm date is confirmed.

SECTION 03How to Check SSC Result 2026 Dhaka Board Online

To check the Dhaka Board result online, go to eboardresults.com, select SSC and 2026, choose Dhaka Board, enter your roll and registration number, and press submit. Checking online is the method most students prefer, because it shows the full marksheet with a grade for every subject, not merely a pass or fail line. Before you begin, keep two documents within reach: your admit card, which carries your six digit roll number, and your registration card, which carries your ten digit registration number. You will need both to unlock the complete grade sheet.

  1. Open an official portal. Visit eboardresults.com or educationboardresults.gov.bd. You can also use the checker on our homepage, which opens the official portal for you.
  2. Select the examination. Choose SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent from the exam list.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Dhaka as your board. This is the step that filters your result to the Dhaka Education Board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number. Type both carefully; a single wrong digit will return an error.
  6. Answer the security check. Solve the simple sum shown to prove you are a real visitor.
  7. Press Get Result. Your marksheet appears on screen. Save it straight away with a screenshot or the print option.

If the website is slow, do not panic. Around two million students refresh the same servers within minutes of 2:00 PM, so a delay is completely normal and does not mean your result is missing. Wait a little, refresh the page, or switch to the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website at all.

SECTION 04Check Dhaka Board Result 2026 by SMS

To check the Dhaka Board result by SMS, type SSC DHA <roll> 2026 and send it to 16222. The SMS method is dependable precisely when the website struggles. It needs no internet connection and works on any handset. Open your message app and send the following to 16222:

SSC DHA <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your own roll number. For example, a Dhaka Board student with roll 123456 would type SSC DHA 123456 2026 and send it to 16222. A return message arrives shortly afterwards carrying your GPA and pass status. A small standard charge applies per SMS. A useful habit: type this message before 2:00 PM and keep it in your drafts, so you can send it the instant results go live.

SECTION 05Dhaka Board SSC Result 2025: How the Board Performed

To set realistic expectations for 2026, it helps to look at what happened last year. In 2025, the Dhaka Board recorded a pass rate of 67.51 percent, a steep fall from the previous year. Out of 3,79,247 students who appeared, 2,56,039 passed, and 37,068 earned the top grade of GPA 5. As across the country, girls led the board: 19,992 female students secured GPA 5 against 17,076 male students, and the female pass rate of 70.36 percent comfortably outpaced the male rate of 64.29 percent.

SECTION 07SSC Result 2026 Dhaka Board with Full Marksheet

To see your Dhaka Board marksheet with the grade in every subject, check online at eboardresults.com and enter both your roll and registration number. The SMS method returns only the GPA, so the online portal is the only way to see subject-wise grades. Many students specifically want the marksheet with number and grade for each subject, not just the overall GPA. This is exactly where the online method beats SMS, because an SMS reply returns only your GPA and pass status. Remember that the marksheet shown online right after publication is the provisional grade sheet. The original printed grade sheet and certificate are distributed later through your school. The good news is that for class XI admission, the online grade sheet is accepted, so you can begin applying without waiting for the printed copy.

A realistic rule of thumb: the most competitive science seats effectively require a GPA of 5.00, while business studies and humanities seats at top colleges usually sit around 4.50 to 5.00. The three colleges that run their own tests, Notre Dame, Holy Cross and St. Joseph, treat GPA 5.00 as the minimum just to apply, with final selection decided by a written test and viva. If any of these is your target, begin preparing for the entrance test right after your exams rather than waiting for the result, because the window between results and the tests is short.

How central Class XI admission works

For the majority of colleges, admission is merit based through the online portal. In short: results are published, the portal opens, you select between five and ten colleges in order of preference using their EIIN codes, you pay the application fee by bKash or Nagad, and you are placed by merit against your GPA and group. Because this year's result is confirmed for 10 August, expect the admission circular to follow shortly after, so have your college shortlist ready in advance.

SECTION 11A Guide for Students Outside Dhaka City

Not every Dhaka Board student lives in the capital, and many students from other boards also aim for Dhaka colleges. If you sat under a different board but want to study in Dhaka, the central admission system lets you apply to Dhaka colleges on the same merit basis, so a strong GPA travels with you. The main practical differences to plan for are accommodation, since many outside students need a hostel or mess, and competition, since Dhaka seats attract the strongest applicants nationwide.

The honest advice for an ambitious student outside the capital is to treat your GPA as your passport. A GPA 5.00 from any board is accepted at Dhaka colleges through the central system, and the elite test based colleges admit strong students regardless of home district. Plan your shortlist across three tiers, a few elite Dhaka names, several strong realistic colleges, and safe options closer to home, and you keep the door to a Dhaka education open without risking a place altogether.

SECTION 12The Long Term Advantage of a Strong Dhaka Result

A strong SSC result under the Dhaka Board carries beyond college admission. The capital's top colleges feed directly into the most competitive university and professional tracks in Bangladesh, from Dhaka University and BUET to the medical and cadet pathways. Students who secure a place at a leading Dhaka college gain access to stronger peer groups, better coaching, and a dense network of alumni and opportunities that compound over the years.

None of this means a result is destiny. Students from every board and every district succeed, and a single exam does not define a career. But it is fair to say that a strong Dhaka Board result opens the widest set of doors at the earliest stage, which is exactly why the admission race that follows the result is worth planning for carefully rather than leaving to chance.

SECTION 13If Your Dhaka Board Result Looks Wrong

To apply for re-scrutiny (board challenge), send an SMS from a Teletalk SIM: type RSC DHA <roll> <subject code> to 16222, then confirm with the PIN you receive. The fee is about 150 Taka per subject. Suppose a subject comes back lower than you were certain of. The Dhaka Board, like every board, allows you to apply for this re-check, often called a board challenge. For the complete process, fees and deadline, read our guide on how to apply for the SSC board challenge. It is important to understand what this is and is not. The board does not re-mark your entire paper from scratch. Instead, it re-checks the mechanics: whether every answer was marked, whether the marks were totalled correctly, and whether the total was carried over accurately. In practice, addition and omission mistakes are the usual reasons a grade changes.

Applications open the day after results and stay open for roughly a week, made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM, with a fee of around 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a genuine reason to doubt a result, since most applications do not change a grade.
