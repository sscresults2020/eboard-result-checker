SECTION 01When Will the Dhaka Board SSC Result 2026 Be Published?

Let us start with the question you came here to answer. The SSC examinations of 2026 were held first, and the results were originally scheduled for 20 July 2026. That date has since been postponed, and the boards now expect to publish in the last week of July. Because the Dhaka Board sits at the centre of the inter board coordination committee, its result is released at the same moment as every other board, at 2:00 PM on the announced day.

Although a delay is frustrating when you have been waiting for weeks, it does not change your marks in any way. What it does change is which "date" you should trust. Every year, unofficial pages circulate confident dates that turn out to be guesses, so please rely on official Dhaka Board notices and reputable news outlets. We update this page the moment a firm date is confirmed.

Please note: the final publication date has not yet been fixed in writing by the Ministry of Education. Treat "last week of July" as a strong expectation rather than a confirmed appointment, and check the announcement bar on our homepage for live updates.

SECTION 02How to Check SSC Result 2026 Dhaka Board Online

Checking online is the method most students prefer, because it shows the full marksheet with a grade for every subject, not merely a pass or fail line. Before you begin, keep two documents within reach: your admit card, which carries your six digit roll number, and your registration card, which carries your ten digit registration number. You will need both to unlock the complete grade sheet.

  1. Open an official portal. Visit eboardresults.com or educationboardresults.gov.bd. You can also use the checker on our homepage, which opens the official portal for you.
  2. Select the examination. Choose SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent from the exam list.
  3. Choose the year 2026.
  4. Select Dhaka as your board. This is the step that filters your result to the Dhaka Education Board.
  5. Enter your roll and registration number. Type both carefully; a single wrong digit will return an error.
  6. Answer the security check. Solve the simple sum shown to prove you are a real visitor.
  7. Press Get Result. Your marksheet appears on screen. Save it straight away with a screenshot or the print option.

If the website is slow, do not panic. Around two million students refresh the same servers within minutes of 2:00 PM, so a delay is completely normal and does not mean your result is missing. Wait a little, refresh the page, or switch to the SMS method below, which does not depend on the website at all.

SECTION 03Check Dhaka Board Result 2026 by SMS

The SMS method is dependable precisely when the website struggles. It needs no internet connection and works on any handset. Open your message app and send the following to 16222:

SSC DHA <ROLL> 2026

Replace <ROLL> with your own roll number. For example, a Dhaka Board student with roll 123456 would type SSC DHA 123456 2026 and send it to 16222. A return message arrives shortly afterwards carrying your GPA and pass status. A small standard charge applies per SMS. A useful habit: type this message before 2:00 PM and keep it in your drafts, so you can send it the instant results go live.

SECTION 04Dhaka Board SSC Result 2025: How the Board Performed

To set realistic expectations for 2026, it helps to look at what happened last year. In 2025, the Dhaka Board recorded a pass rate of 67.51 percent, a steep fall from the previous year. Out of 3,79,247 students who appeared, 2,56,039 passed, and 37,068 earned the top grade of GPA 5. As across the country, girls led the board: 19,992 female students secured GPA 5 against 17,076 male students, and the female pass rate of 70.36 percent comfortably outpaced the male rate of 64.29 percent.

SECTION 06SSC Result 2026 Dhaka Board with Full Marksheet

Many students specifically want the marksheet with number and grade for each subject, not just the overall GPA. This is exactly where the online method beats SMS, because an SMS reply returns only your GPA and pass status. To see the complete grade sheet, follow the online steps in Section 02 and be sure to enter both your roll and registration number. The individual result page then lists every compulsory and elective subject with its letter grade and grade point, followed by your final GPA.

Remember that the marksheet shown online right after publication is the provisional grade sheet. The original printed grade sheet and certificate are distributed later through your school. The good news is that for class XI admission, the online grade sheet is accepted, so you can begin applying without waiting for the printed copy.

SECTION 07If Your Dhaka Board Result Looks Wrong

Suppose a subject comes back lower than you were certain of. The Dhaka Board, like every board, allows you to apply for re-scrutiny, often called a board challenge. It is important to understand what this is and is not. The board does not re-mark your entire paper from scratch. Instead, it re-checks the mechanics: whether every answer was marked, whether the marks were totalled correctly, and whether the total was carried over accurately. In practice, addition and omission mistakes are the usual reasons a grade changes.

Applications open the day after results and stay open for roughly a week, made by SMS from a Teletalk SIM, with a fee of around 125 to 150 Taka per subject. Apply only where you have a genuine reason to doubt a result, since most applications do not change a grade.
